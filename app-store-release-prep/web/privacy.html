Privacy Policy

Short version: your desk data stays on your iPhone.

Effective 28 August 2026

Standing Desk controls a compatible standing desk directly from your iPhone over Bluetooth Low Energy.

Data collection

Standing Desk does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or server connection.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the selected desk identifier and name, height configuration, Sit and Stand presets, and safety acknowledgement on your iPhone. This information remains inside the app’s local storage.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to discover a compatible desk, read its height, and control movement while the app is open. Bluetooth communication is direct between your iPhone and the desk.

Deleting data

Use Forget Desk to remove the selected desk. Delete the app to remove all locally stored app data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes

This policy will change if a future release changes how the app handles data. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, email cristiancaroli@gmail.com or use the support page.
